# Thanks for the Buff (TFTB)

Auto thank every player who buffs you with a whisper or emote, and thank anyone by hand with a click of your Thank You macros. Manners, automated.

**TL;DR:** Be the friendly face in Azeroth without breaking stride. Strangers who buff you get thanked on the spot, and any thank-you you owe is one click away.

### Stranger Buffs

* **Every Buff Gets a Thank-You** // Strangers who buff you in passing get a friendly emote, automatically, so no kindness goes unnoticed.
* **Never Spammy** // A wave of world buffs or a generous repeat buffer never turns your gratitude into spam.

### Thank You Button

* **Thank Anyone** // Someone tossed you Fortitude, Arcane Intellect, or Mark of the Wild? Target them, and your Thank You macro emotes your thanks.
* **Five Buttons, Your Way** // Each has its own emote and icon: thank a fly-by buffer, ask a mage for water, or heckle a guildie.

## Setup

1. Install the add-on, ideally using [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/thanks-for-the-buff-revisited) or [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/thanks-for-the-buff).
2. Type `/tftb` to open the Options Interface.
3. On Stranger Buffs, pick the emotes you like.
4. Open your Macros window and drag `- Thank` onto your action bar.
5. _"Manners maketh the raid."_
