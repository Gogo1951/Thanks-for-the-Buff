# Thanks for the Buff // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff thanks players outside your group who buff you, waves at friends and guildmates you pass, and gives you Thank You macros to thank anyone by hand. This time we most want you to try the new Wave to Friends.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Type `/tftb`, open **Wave to Friends**, turn on **Enable Wave to Friends**, then mouse over a friend or guildmate in town; you wave at them.
2. Mouse over that player again; nothing happens until the **Same-Player Cooldown** runs out.
3. Mouse over a friend outside town; nothing happens until you turn off **Only While Resting**.
4. Mouse over a friend in your group, then one while you're in combat; no wave either time.
5. On the **Guilds** tab, add another guild's name and mouse over one of its members; you wave at them.
6. On the **Custom List** tab, add a friend set to **None** and someone else with their own emote; the friend gets nothing and the other gets their emote (on WoW Forever, also try a first name alone).

## Everything Else

7. On **Give Thanks**, turn on **Enable Print Outs** and **Enable Sound Effects**, then let a stranger buff you in a capital city; you emote at them, see a line in chat and hear a sound.
8. Have a groupmate buff you; nothing should happen, since they aren't a stranger.
9. On WoW Forever, get buffed by a stranger **in combat**; no error should appear.
10. On **Ignored Buffs**, add a buff with **Ignore Current Buff** or **Add Buff**, then get it from a stranger; no thank-you goes out, but your print out and sound still do.
11. On **Thank You Button**, tick **Enable Macro "- Thank"** and press the macro on a target (on WoW Forever, **in combat** too).
12. Type `/tftb` and check the options open inside the game's Options, especially on TBC Anniversary; in combat, it should refuse.
