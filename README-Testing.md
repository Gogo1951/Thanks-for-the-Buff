# Thanks for the Buff (TFTB) // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff (TFTB) thanks the players who buff you, tells the players you buff what they got, and alerts you to feasts, portals and same-class cooldowns. This time we most want you to try Buff Duty, a new window that shows who in your group is missing a buff you can give.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On a Mage, Priest or Druid, join a group and click the icons in the new Buff Duty window to buff whoever is missing one.
2. Under **Buff Duty**, turn on **Return the Favor**, let a stranger buff you, and click their icon to buff them back (right-click takes them off the list).
3. Turn on **Your Target** and target a player outside your group who is missing your buff; they should show up in the window.
4. Turn on **Your Self-Buffs** and let your armor, aura or Inner Fire run out; it should appear at the top of the window.
5. Untick **Hide in Combat** and fight with the window showing, especially on WoW Forever, where nothing should error.
6. On a Warrior or Rogue, open **Buff Duty** in the options; it should say your class has no buffs.
7. Open the new **Tracked Abilities** page under **Teammate Buffs**, **Send Good News**, **Service Alerts** or **Peer Pressure** and switch a few abilities off and on.

## Everything Else

8. Stand in a capital city and let a stranger buff you; you emote at them.
9. With **Teammate Buffs** on, have a groupmate cast Power Infusion or Innervate on you, and on WoW Forever do it **in combat** too.
10. With **Service Alerts** on, have a group member set out a feast, drop a soulwell or open a portal.
11. With **Send Good News** on, cast a buff such as Power Infusion on another player, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat** too.
12. On Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, have a groupmate of your own class use a big cooldown to set off **Peer Pressure**.
13. On **Thank You Button**, tick **Enable Macro "- Thank"**, target a player and press the macro, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat** too.
14. Type `/tftb` and check the options open inside the Blizzard Options interface, especially on TBC Anniversary, then try it in combat; it should refuse.
