# Thanks for the Buff // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff thanks the players who buff you, tells the players you buff what they got, alerts you to feasts, portals and same-class cooldowns, and shows who's missing a buff you can give in its Buff Duty window. This time we most want you to try Buff Duty's new self-buffs, weapon buffs and buff keys.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On a Warrior, Shadow Priest or Demonic Sacrifice Warlock, let Battle Shout, Shadowform or your sacrifice drop and click it back up from **Self-Buffs** at the top of the Buff Duty window.
2. On a Warlock in a group where nobody is soulstoned, click the Soulstone in the window; it should go on a healer, or make a stone if you have none.
3. On any class, let a poison, imbue, oil, stone or fishing lure wear off and click it back onto the right weapon from that hand's row (on TBC Anniversary, try a different one per hand); unticked under **Buff Duty → Weapon Buffs**, it should stop appearing.
4. Under **Buff Duty → Keybinds**, bind the **Individual Buff Button** and press it out of combat, one buff per press, until it says "Nothing left to buff."
5. Bind the **Group Buff Button** and press it with one party member far away; that party should wait while the others get their buff.
6. Put `/click TFTBBuffIndividual` in a macro, check it casts what the key does, and check neither casts anything in combat.
7. Under **Buff Duty → Tracked Abilities**, set a class under **Count as Tanks** to **Always**; its players should be offered your Tanks buffs, like Thorns.
8. On **Thank You Button**, tick **Enable Macro "- Thank"**, pick an **Icon**, and press the macro on a target (on WoW Forever, **in combat** too).

## Everything Else

9. On a Mage, Priest or Druid, join a group and click the icons in the Buff Duty window to buff whoever is missing one; on WoW Forever, untick **Hide in Combat** and fight with it showing.
10. Stand in a capital city and let a stranger buff you; you emote at them.
11. With **Teammate Buffs** on, have a groupmate cast Power Infusion or Innervate on you, and on WoW Forever do it **in combat** too.
12. With **Service Alerts** on, have a group member set out a feast, drop a soulwell or open a portal.
13. With **Send Good News** on, buff another player (on WoW Forever, **in combat** too); only short buffs like Power Infusion should say how long they last.
14. On Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, have a groupmate of your own class use a big cooldown to set off **Peer Pressure**.
15. Type `/tftb` and check the options open inside the game's Options, especially on TBC Anniversary, then try it in combat; it should refuse.
