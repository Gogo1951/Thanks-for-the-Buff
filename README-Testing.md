# Thanks for the Buff // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff thanks strangers who buff you, tells players what you just cast on them, waves at friends and guildmates you pass, and gives you Thank You macros to thank anyone by hand. This time we most want you to try the new **mini-map menu** and **Return Buff**, a macro that casts your buff back on whoever just buffed you.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Hover the TFTB mini-map button to open its menu, then Right-Click the button to turn Give Thanks on and off and Middle-Click for Wave to Friends; the menu's On and Off follow.
2. In the menu, hover **Give Thanks** and click a buff under **Recent Buffs** to ignore it, then click it under **Ignored Buffs** to stop ignoring it; chat says so each time.
3. On **Return Buff** (under Give Thanks), tick **Enable Macro "- Return Buff"**, put it on your bar, get buffed by a stranger and press it; your buff goes back on them, at a rank their level can take.
4. On WoW Forever, get buffed by a stranger **in combat**; no error appears, and **- Return Buff** aims at them once combat ends.
5. On **Give Thanks**, fill in all three **Whisper Messages** boxes; strangers who buff you get one of them at random.
6. On **Thank You Button**, click **Pick Up Macro**, drop **- Thank** on your bar, put `{name}` in its whisper and press it on a target; the whisper names them.

## Everything Else

7. Turn on **Enable Print Outs** and **Enable Sound Effects**, then get buffed by a stranger and by a groupmate; only the stranger gets thanked, and you see a line and hear a sound.
8. Equip a fishing pole, or start a long cast, and have a stranger buff you; you don't emote and your cast isn't interrupted.
9. On **Good News**, turn on **Enable Good News** and cast a buff like Power Infusion or a Blessing on another player; they get a whisper naming it (WoW Forever has no Good News).
10. On WoW Forever, press **- Thank** on a target **in combat**; you emote and no error appears.
11. On **Wave to Friends**, turn on **Enable Wave to Friends** and mouse over a friend or guildmate in town; you wave at them.
12. Type `/tftb`, then click **TFTB Options** in the mini-map menu; both open the options inside the game's Options (watch TBC Anniversary), and in combat they refuse with a message.
