# Thanks for the Buff // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff thanks strangers who buff you, tells players what you just cast on them, waves at friends and guildmates you pass, and gives you macros to thank anyone or return a buff. This time we most want you to try **Good News** in the mini-map menu and the new **Return the Favor** page.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. In the TFTB mini-map menu, turn **Good News** on, hover it, pick **Who Gets It** and untick an ability; the Good News options show the same picks (WoW Forever has no Good News).
2. Middle-Click the mini-map button and nothing happens, then Right-Click a menu row; it does what a Left-Click does.
3. Open **Return the Favor**, now its own page beside Give Thanks, tick **Enable Macro "- Return Buff"**, get buffed by a stranger and press it; your buff goes back on them (on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, at a rank their level can take).
4. Turn on friendly nameplates and get buffed by a stranger who isn't your target; your thank-you emote is aimed at them (watch WoW Forever).
5. Hold the menu open and tick an emote on **Give Thanks > Emotes List**; the menu's **Choose Emotes** count changes at once.

## Everything Else

6. Turn on **Enable Print Outs** and **Enable Sound Effects**, then get buffed by a stranger and by a groupmate; only the stranger gets thanked, with a line and a sound.
7. Equip a fishing pole, or start a long cast, and have a stranger buff you; you don't emote and your cast isn't interrupted.
8. On WoW Forever, get buffed by a stranger **in combat** and press **- Thank** on a target; no error appears, and **- Return Buff** aims at the stranger once combat ends.
9. On **Good News**, turn on **Enable Good News** and cast a buff like Power Infusion or a Blessing on another player; they get a whisper naming it.
10. On **Thank You Button**, click **Pick Up Macro**, drop **- Thank** on your bar, put `{name}` in its whisper and press it on a target; the whisper names them.
11. On **Wave to Friends**, turn on **Enable Wave to Friends** and mouse over a friend or guildmate in town; you wave at them.
12. Type `/tftb`, then click **TFTB Options** in the mini-map menu; both open the options inside the game's Options (watch TBC Anniversary), and in combat they refuse with a message.
