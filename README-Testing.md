# Thanks for the Buff (TFTB) // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff (TFTB) thanks the players who buff you with emotes, whispers and chat lines, and alerts you to feasts, portals and same-class cooldowns. This time we most want you to try it on WoW Forever, which it now supports.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On a character that has never used TFTB, type `/tftb` and switch features on from the new **Features** section of the main page (only Stranger Buffs starts on).
2. Ask a friend to check that your thank-you and Good News whispers now end with "// TFTB" instead of starting with it.
3. Click the speaker icon beside any **Enable Sound Effects** box to hear the sound before you turn it on.

## Everything Else

4. Stand in a capital city and let a stranger buff you; you emote at them.
5. With Teammate Buffs on, have a groupmate cast Power Infusion or Innervate on you, and on WoW Forever do it **in combat** too (it's skipped there, but nothing should error).
6. With Service Alerts on, have a group member set out a feast, drop a soulwell or open a portal.
7. With Good News on, cast a buff such as Power Infusion on another player, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat** too.
8. On Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, have a groupmate of your own class use a big cooldown to set off Peer Pressure.
9. On **Thank You Button**, tick **Enable Macro "- Thank"**, target a player and press the macro, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat** too.
10. Type `/tftb` and check the options open inside the Blizzard Options interface, especially on TBC Anniversary.
11. Type `/tftb` during combat; it should refuse with a chat message.
