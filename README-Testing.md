# Thanks for the Buff // Test Plan

Thanks for the Buff thanks the players who buff you, tells the players you buff what they got, alerts you to feasts, portals and soulwells, and shows who's missing a buff you can give in its Buff Duty window. This time we most want you to try Buff Duty's new **When to Show** settings and its single **Buff Button**.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Under **Buff Duty → When to Show**, set **Solo** and **In Group** to **Always** or **In Instance**; the window should only show where you picked (by default, in a group it waits for a dungeon, raid or battleground).
2. Close the Buff Duty window with the X, check it stays gone on this class but still shows on your other characters, then Shift + Right-Click the mini-map button to bring it back; on a Paladin it starts off.
3. Under **Buff Duty → Keybinds**, bind the **Buff Button** (or keep the key you had on the old Group Buff Button) and press it out of combat until it says "Nothing left to buff."; with most of a party in range it should cast the group buff, with one player missing, the single one.
4. Tick **Skip Buffs with Cooldowns** on a Priest with Fear Ward offered; the key should pass over Fear Ward and move on.
5. Find a groupmate wearing a weaker version of a buff you give, like a lower rank of Arcane Intellect; the window should offer yours, but not when theirs is stronger.
6. With **Teammate Buffs** on, have a groupmate cast Power Infusion or Innervate on you (on WoW Forever, **in combat** too); you should hear a sound and nothing should print.
7. On any version but WoW Forever, open the **Peer Pressure** tab; it should say the feature moved to Control Freak, with a link to copy.

## Everything Else

8. On a Mage, Priest or Druid, join a group and click the icons in the Buff Duty window to buff whoever is missing one; on WoW Forever, untick **Hide in Combat** and fight with it showing.
9. On a Warrior or Shadow Priest let Battle Shout or Shadowform drop, and on a Rogue or Shaman let a poison or imbue wear off, then click each back on from the top of the window.
10. Stand in a capital city and let a stranger buff you; you emote at them.
11. With **Service Alerts** on, have a group member set out a feast, drop a soulwell or open a portal.
12. With **Send Good News** on, buff another player (on WoW Forever, **in combat** too); only short buffs like Power Infusion should say how long they last.
13. On **Thank You Button**, tick **Enable Macro "- Thank"** and press the macro on a target (on WoW Forever, **in combat** too).
14. Type `/tftb`, and Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, and check the options open inside the game's Options, especially on TBC Anniversary; in combat, both should refuse.
